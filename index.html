ABOUT

Get ready for a year of exciting competition in the world of Uma Musume: Pretty Derby! The TDS Grand Prix is a year-long tournament designed to challenge your skills, strategy, and dedication. Whether you're a seasoned pro or a new trainer, this circuit is for you!

Tadano Sho is a grand prix with a legacy stretching back to 2023. This new revival brings a new format that is not just efficient and scalable, but more open to more participants, both casual and competitive players.
